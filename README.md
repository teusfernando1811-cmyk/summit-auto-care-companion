# Summit Auto Care Companion

Create a complete high-quality MULTI-PAGE website for a fictional independent US automotive repair shop called "Summit Auto Repair", based in Des Moines, Iowa. This is a polished $800-$1500 agency-quality local-service business site, not SaaS. Use the most recently discussed reference: white, deep navy, restrained red accent, large authentic mechanic-at-work hero photo, professional traditional American repair-shop feel. Make it LESS cluttered/informational than the provided earlier mockup. Strong typographic hierarchy, generous intentional whitespace, beautiful real photographic content, smart responsive layouts, subtle transitions, no dashboard UI, NO repetitive AI-generated card grids, NO generic gradients, NO emoji icons, NO excessive badges, no exaggerated claims. Distinctive simple mountain-style wordmark "SUMMIT AUTO REPAIR" made with CSS/SVG, elegant navigation. US ENGLISH copy throughout. Build genuinely navigable pages with working routing: Home (/), Services (/services), About (/about), Reviews (/reviews), Contact (/contact), and Book Appointment (/appointment). Top navbar and footer linked, mobile menu that really works. Home should be concise: attractive full-width hero with headline "Straightforward repairs. A shop you can trust.", strong Book Appointment & Call CTA, compact trust-oriented statement, 3 featured services with high-quality photos or carefully styled blocks, a human and authentic About teaser, one honest testimonial placeholder properly marked sample, and concise location/contact CTA. Other content lives on separate pages. Services page has oil changes, brakes, diagnostics, tires & alignments, engine repair, suspension, with clear descriptions, not overwhelming. About page conveys craftsmanship, local service, transparency, images. Reviews page gracefully includes clearly labeled sample testimonial placeholders, never imply they're real reviews or show fabricated star counts. Contact page with click-to-call, business hours, a map link for Des Moines (not an invented street address), and a usable contact form with clear client-side validation and an honest demo success state (no fake backend or promise of actual delivery). Appointment page with elegant form (name, email, phone, vehicle, service, date, optional notes), validation and explicitly DEMO ONLY confirmation with no real booking/integration. Do not invent actual phone numbers, certifications, warranties, street address, endorsements or business history; use editable visibly identified placeholder phone and business details where needed, avoid fake social proof. Prefer a subtle in-site demo note rather than polluting polished presentation. Visual style intentional: editorial layout for local automotive trade, imagery of real shops/mechanics/cars, navy #101d2c, red #bb1725, warm off-white #f7f7f4, clean font pairing, natural sections and asymmetry. Provide SEO titles/meta, accessibility semantic tags, working nav/buttons, keyboard states, responsive mobile, performant lazy images. The site should look human-designed by an experienced boutique web agency, premium and bespoke, not a default AI landing page or a software product.
